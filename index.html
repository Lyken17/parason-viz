One problem, both kinds of parallelism

To find ab + ba, the model needs two roots that don't depend on each other, so it forks into two Subtask threads, and both results are required. Neither root is known in advance, so each subtask forks again into Trial threads that test candidate roots at the same time. One hit is enough, and the dead ends stay in context. Format and terms follow the Parason paper.
